It's Your Railroad After All. Make the App YOURS!

Make the app feel like your railroad.

The new Custom Splash Screen feature lets you choose the image, background color, display time, and entrance animation shown when the app starts.

A personalized startup experience

The app can now show a configurable in-app splash screen after the normal device launch screen and before the existing startup flow. Turn it on for a branded, personalized welcome screen, or turn it off to continue directly into the app.

Enable or disable

You control whether it appears

Use the Enable Custom Splash switch to turn the feature on or off. When disabled, the app skips the custom splash and continues normally.

Custom image

Choose your own square image

Select a photo or logo from your device. Images must be square so they display consistently across different phones and screen sizes.

Background color

Match your theme

Pick the background color that surrounds the image, allowing the splash to match your railroad, club, or preferred app theme.

Display time

Choose how long it remains visible

Set the splash duration in milliseconds. The default is 4000 ms, with a minimum supported time of 500 ms.

Animation

Select how the splash appears

Choose no animation, have the splash grow outward from the center with From Singularity, or have it scale inward from an oversized view with From Infinity.

Test button

Preview it before restarting

Tap Test Splash Screen to see the current image, color, timing, and animation without restarting the app. When the preview ends, you return to the same settings screen.

The selected image is displayed centered and aspect-fit, so it keeps its original shape without stretching or distortion.

How to set it up

Everything is available from Global Settings → Appearance → Custom Splash Screen.

1

Enable the feature

Turn on Enable Custom Splash to reveal the available options.

2

Choose a square image

Tap Upload and select the image you want to use from your device.

3

Pick the background

Select a background color that complements your image.

4

Set the timing

Choose how long the splash should remain visible during startup.

5

Choose the animation

Select None, From Singularity, or From Infinity.

6

Preview it

Use Test Splash Screen to confirm the look before your next app launch.

Custom Splash Screen gives every PMT user a simple way to make the app feel more personal from the moment it opens.
