PMT Camera Overlay

Record the railroad without giving up control of the train.

PMT solves one of the most frustrating problems model railroaders report with other systems: having to leave the throttle app and switch to the phone’s camera just to record video.

The problem with switching apps

Railroaders using other control systems often say the same thing: the moment they open the camera, they lose immediate access to throttle, sound, lighting, braking, and direction controls. That turns a simple video into a juggling act.

The old experience

Choose between filming and operating

Switching to the camera app means the train keeps moving while the operator no longer has the throttle in front of them.

The PMT experience

Film and operate at the same time

PMT lets the camera float directly over the throttle screen so you can see the live recording while continuing to control the train underneath it.

No more deciding whether to capture the moment or control the train. With PMT, you can do both.

Innovation without the premium-system price

High-priced systems often ask users to accept workflow limitations that model railroaders have complained about for years. PMT takes a different approach: listen to the real frustrations and build practical solutions directly into the ecosystem.
