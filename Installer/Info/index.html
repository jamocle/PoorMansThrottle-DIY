What is PMT? Poor Man’s Throttle is a completely FREE DIY wireless train control system for DC and dead-rail (Battery) locomotives. It uses affordable ESP32-based hardware and a phone app so builders can run, tune, and improve their railroad without expensive proprietary gear.
