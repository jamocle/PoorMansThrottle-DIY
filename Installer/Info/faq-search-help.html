FAQ Search Help

Search the FAQ with words, phrases, and Boolean operators.

FAQ searches are case-insensitive. Use a simple word search for everyday questions, or combine terms with AND, OR, NOT, parentheses, and quoted phrases when you need more control.

Basic searches

Separate words are treated as AND. Both words must appear somewhere in the same FAQ, but they do not need to be next to each other or in the same order.

motor driver Matches FAQs that contain both motor and driver.
"motor driver" Matches the exact phrase motor driver.

Searches are case-insensitive, so motor, Motor, and MOTOR are equivalent.

Boolean operators

  • AND — both expressions must match.
  • OR — either expression may match.
  • NOT — excludes FAQs that match the following expression.
  • ( ) — groups expressions and controls how they are evaluated.
  • " " — searches for an exact phrase.

Operator precedence is parentheses first, then NOT, then AND, then OR. Operator names are also case-insensitive.

(motor AND driver) OR IBT OR L298 Matches FAQs with both motor and driver, or FAQs containing IBT, or FAQs containing L298.
sound AND (DFPlayer OR PMTPlayer) Requires sound plus either DFPlayer or PMTPlayer.
LED AND NOT WS2812 Requires LED and excludes FAQs containing WS2812.

Quoted text and special characters

Inside a quoted phrase, AND, OR, NOT, and parentheses are ordinary text. Quoted phrases preserve punctuation and still match case-insensitively.

"test(me)" Searches literally for test(me).
"test AND me" Searches literally for the words test AND me; AND is not an operator here.

Escaping quotes and backslashes

Inside quoted phrases, use \" for a literal quotation mark and \\ for a literal backslash.

"\"test\"" Searches for the literal text "test", including the quotation marks.
"\\" Searches for one literal backslash.
"\\\"" Searches for the exact two-character sequence \".

Reserved search syntax

  • AND, OR, and NOT are operators when used as standalone words outside quotes.
  • ( and ) group expressions outside quotes.
  • " starts or ends a quoted phrase.
  • \ is the escape character inside quoted phrases.

Other punctuation such as hyphens, underscores, periods, slashes, colons, plus signs, number signs, and ampersands remains normal searchable text.

Invalid expressions

If the expression is incomplete or malformed, the FAQ shows a validation message and leaves the current results unchanged. Examples include an unmatched quotation mark, a missing closing parenthesis, or a Boolean operator with a missing term.

motor AND (driver OR Invalid because the expression is incomplete and the closing parenthesis is missing.
""" Invalid because the quotation marks do not form a valid non-empty quoted phrase.
""test"" Invalid because empty quoted phrases are not allowed.
