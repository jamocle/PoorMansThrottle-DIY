More than a master volume.

Shape the sound of each locomotive the way you want it. Individual sound groups can now be adjusted independently, giving you fine control over the balance between the prime mover, horn, bell, brakes, chuffs, and other effects.

Build a sound mix that fits the locomotive.

A single master volume is useful, but it cannot account for the way different sound effects compete with one another. Individual sound control lets you tune the mix itself—so the horn can cut through without making the prime mover overwhelming, the bell can sit naturally in the background, and steam effects can be balanced to match the character of the locomotive.

Why individual sound levels matter.

Real locomotives are not made up of equally loud sounds. Some effects should dominate, some should sit behind the action, and some only need to be noticeable at the right moment. Independent level control makes it possible to recreate that balance.
